# Google Merchant Center Setup

## Product Category

Health & Beauty > Health Care > Fitness & Nutrition > Vitamins & Supplements

## Required Product Data

- Product title
- Product description using structure/function language only
- Product URL
- Image URL
- Price
- Availability
- GTIN or identifier exists setting
- Shipping policy
- Return policy
- Brand: Luma Daily

## Product Feed Readiness

For Shopify-native product feeds, confirm these exist in Shopify admin:

- Luma Energy
- Luma Calm
- Luma Sleep
- Luma Glow
- Luma Gut

## Compliance

Do not use disease, treatment, prevention, or cure claims in feed titles or descriptions.

Allowed examples:

- Supports energy + focus
- Supports calm + stress balance
- Supports restful sleep
- Supports healthy skin, hair + nails
- Supports digestive wellness
