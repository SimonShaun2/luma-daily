# Directory Listings — Luma Daily

The directories that move the needle for brand entity SEO. Each is ranked by SEO impact (how much it contributes to Knowledge Graph confidence + branded SERP real estate). Set up in priority order.

---

## Tier 1 — Mandatory (set up first 30 days)

### 1. Crunchbase
**URL:** crunchbase.com/organization/luma-daily *(claim once created)*
**Why it matters:** Crunchbase is one of Google's primary sources for organization entity data. Listings appear in Knowledge Panels.
**What to include:**
- Logo, founding date (2026), HQ location, parent company (Luma Wellness Group), industry (Health & Wellness, Dietary Supplements), founders, funding rounds (if any), website.

### 2. LinkedIn Company Page
**URL:** linkedin.com/company/luma-daily
**Why it matters:** Direct sameAs target from Organization schema. Appears in Knowledge Panels. Influences employer-side searches.
**What to include:** All canonical entity facts from [brand-entity-profile.md](brand-entity-profile.md). Industry, company size, founded year, specialties, website.

### 3. Trustpilot
**URL:** trustpilot.com/review/takelumadaily.com
**Why it matters:** Trustpilot reviews appear in branded SERPs. High-volume positive reviews defend brand search results from negative aggregators.
**What to include:** Verified business profile. Encourage organic reviews via post-purchase email after 21-day mark.

### 4. Google Business Profile
**URL:** business.google.com
**Why it matters:** Even for DTC brands without retail presence, GBP enables Knowledge Panel data + brand image carousel in branded SERPs.
**See:** [google-business-profile.md](google-business-profile.md) for full setup spec.

### 5. Wikidata
**URL:** wikidata.org
**Why it matters:** Wikidata feeds Knowledge Graphs across Google, Bing, ChatGPT, and most AI search engines. A clean Wikidata entry is one of the highest-leverage SEO actions a brand can take.
**What to include:** Brand entity (Q-number), founder, parent company, founded date, headquarters, products (each with their own Wikidata entry if eligible), social media handles.

## Standard listing format (use across all directories)

```
Brand Name: Luma Daily
Legal Entity: Luma Wellness Group
Founded: 2026
Headquarters: United States
Industry: Health & Wellness / Dietary Supplements
Sub-category: Functional gummy supplements
Website: https://takelumadaily.com
Email: hello@takelumadaily.com (or directory-specific)
Phone: [if applicable]
Description (50 words): Luma Daily is a premium wellness gummy brand built around the daily ritual — five clean formulas for energy, calm, sleep, glow, and gut support. Transparent dosing, third-party tested, designed for adults building a sustainable wellness routine. Made in the USA.
Logo: https://takelumadaily.com/images/logo.png
Social: Instagram @lumadaily, TikTok @lumadaily, etc.
```

Use this exact format across every directory. The cross-directory consistency is what builds Knowledge Graph confidence.

## What to track

For each listing:
- Date submitted
- Date verified
- Listing URL
- Whether it appears in branded SERP for "luma daily"
- Whether it's cited by AI search engines

After 60 days, run a branded SERP screenshot and verify which directory listings are surfacing. Optimize the ones that aren't ranking by enriching profile content.
