# Google Business Profile — Luma Daily

Even for a DTC e-commerce brand without a physical retail location, Google Business Profile (GBP) provides important brand entity signals and Knowledge Panel real estate for branded SERPs.

---

## Should a DTC brand even have a GBP?

Yes — but configured for the brand's actual business model. GBP supports two primary listing types relevant to Luma Daily:

1. **"Storefront" with no in-person service** — brand HQ address, but `customer service hours only` rather than retail hours. Best for DTC brands with a known HQ city.
2. **"Service-area business"** — no public address, just a service-area definition. Best for fully-remote or warehouse-only operations.

**Recommendation for Luma Daily:** Set up as a service-area business with the United States as the primary service area. This avoids the foot-traffic expectations of a storefront listing while still capturing the Knowledge Panel + branded SERP real estate.

## GBP setup checklist

### Step 1 — Create the listing
- Go to business.google.com → Add business
- Business name: **Luma Daily**
- Business category: **Vitamin & supplements store** (primary)
- Additional categories: **Health and wellness service** *(secondary)*

### Step 2 — Service area
- Hide physical address
- Service area: United States (or expand to Canada + UK as expansion lands)

### Step 3 — Business details

**Contact info:**
- Website: https://takelumadaily.com
- Phone: [add when set up]
- Customer service email: support@takelumadaily.com

**Hours:**
- Customer service hours (e.g., Monday-Friday 9am-5pm PT)
- "Open 24 hours" for online ordering note

**Description (750 char max):**
> Luma Daily is a premium wellness gummy brand built around the daily ritual — five clean formulas for energy, calm, sleep, glow, and gut support. Every formula uses transparent, third-party-tested ingredients at clinically-relevant doses, with no proprietary blends. Available individually or as bundles, with a 20% subscription discount and free shipping. Take the 60-second Find My Ritual quiz at takelumadaily.com to get a personalized formula stack. Made in the USA in cGMP-certified facilities.

**Attributes:**
- Online ordering: yes
- Online care: yes
- Identifies as: women-owned / Black-owned / etc. *(if applicable)*

### Step 4 — Photos
Upload at minimum:
- Logo (square, 1080x1080)
- Cover photo (landscape, 1024x576) — hero brand shot
- 6-10 product photos (one per SKU + bundles)
- 2-3 lifestyle shots (someone taking the gummy, ritual moment)

### Step 5 — Products
Add each product as a separate GBP product:
- Luma Energy ($27.99 / $22.39 sub)
- Luma Calm ($27.99 / $22.39 sub)
- Luma Sleep ($24.99 / $19.99 sub)
- Luma Glow ($29.99 / $23.99 sub)
- Luma Gut ($24.99 / $19.99 sub)
- Daily Ritual Bundle ($139.99 / $111.99 sub)

Each product gets: image, name, price, short description, link to PDP.

### Step 6 — Posts
GBP supports recurring posts (similar to social posts) that appear in branded SERPs.

**Recommended cadence:** 1 post per week.

**Post types to use:**
- Offer (subscription discount, seasonal promo)
- Update (new article, new bundle, blog post)
- Event (product launch, founder podcast appearance)

### Step 7 — Reviews
Encourage reviews via post-purchase email at the 21-day mark. Respond to every review (positive and negative) within 48 hours. GBP review density and recency are ranking factors for branded queries.

### Step 8 — Q&A
GBP supports a Q&A section. Pre-seed with 5-10 questions and answers from [homepage-faq.md](../faq/homepage-faq.md). This prevents random users from posting unanswered questions that show up in SERP.

---

## Verification

GBP requires verification — typically by mail (postcard with code), phone, or video. For a service-area business, video verification is fastest.

---

## What good looks like at day 90

- Profile complete (all fields filled)
- 50+ photos uploaded
- 10+ posts published
- 25+ verified reviews (4.5+ average)
- Q&A section pre-seeded with 10+ Q&As
- Appears in Knowledge Panel for "luma daily" branded search
- Appears in image carousel for "luma daily" SERP
