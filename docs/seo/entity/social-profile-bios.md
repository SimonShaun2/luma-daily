## Why bio consistency matters for SEO

Google's Knowledge Graph builds entity confidence by cross-referencing brand mentions across social profiles. When Luma Daily's bio, founding date, parent company, and category are identical across Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, and Pinterest, Google increases its confidence that this is a coherent real brand entity — which:

- Surfaces the brand in Knowledge Panels for branded searches
- Improves AI Overview attribution (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI cite "established" brands more confidently)
- Generates `sameAs` schema validation across the brand's social `Organization` markup
- Increases branded-search CTR by surfacing rich brand data in SERPs
