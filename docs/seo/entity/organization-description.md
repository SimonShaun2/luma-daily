## 350-word version (full press kit)

Luma Daily is a premium wellness gummy brand founded in 2026 by Luma Wellness Group, built for adults who want a daily wellness routine that's calm, considered, and actually consistent.

The brand emerged from a simple observation about the wellness category: most consumers who buy supplements abandon them within 30 days, the average wellness stack is six bottles deep and unsustainable, and the industry's response has been to escalate hype, dose, and packaging volume rather than solve the consistency problem. Luma Daily was built as a response — a five-formula daily ritual designed for the long term, with the brand restraint and editorial voice of a premium wellness publication.

The product line consists of five complementary gummy formulas: Luma Energy (morning, with ginseng, B12, and CoQ10), Luma Calm (midday, with KSM-66 ashwagandha and L-theanine), Luma Sleep (evening, with low-dose 3mg melatonin and chamomile), Luma Glow (always-on beauty support, with marine collagen and biotin), and Luma Gut (always-on digestive support, with 10 billion CFU probiotics and inulin prebiotic). The formulas are designed without ingredient overlap, allowing customers to take any combination together as part of a complete daily wellness ritual.

Luma Daily distinguishes itself in several ways: every dose is listed exactly as formulated (no proprietary blends), every batch is third-party tested by an independent ISO-certified lab, and ingredient choices favor evidence-backed amounts over trace inclusion. The brand also operates a 60-second Find My Ritual quiz that recommends a personalized two-to-three formula stack based on customer goals — and a flexible subscription model that includes 20% off, free shipping, and the ability to pause, skip, or cancel anytime.

The brand is direct-to-consumer at takelumadaily.com, available in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. Manufacturing is done in cGMP-certified facilities in the United States. Luma Daily is owned by Luma Wellness Group.
