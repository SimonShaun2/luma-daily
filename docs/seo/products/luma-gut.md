## Ingredient explainer copy

**Probiotics — 10 Billion CFU.** A blend of clinically-studied probiotic strains designed for adult digestive support. CFU (colony-forming units) measures the number of viable bacteria per serving. 10 billion is in the effective daily range research suggests for general gut wellness support.

**Inulin Prebiotic — 500mg.** A soluble fiber sourced from chicory root. Inulin doesn't get digested in the stomach — it travels to the large intestine where it feeds beneficial bacteria. This is the "synbiotic" approach: probiotics + prebiotics taken together work better than either alone.

**Digestive Enzymes — 200mg.** A blend of enzymes that support the breakdown of proteins (proteases), fats (lipases), and carbohydrates (amylases) — the three macronutrients in food. Helps your body extract nutrients more efficiently and reduces digestive load.

**Ginger Root — 150mg.** A traditional botanical used for thousands of years to support digestive comfort. Modern research suggests Ginger may help support normal gut motility and ease occasional discomfort.

*No proprietary blends. Every dose is listed exactly as formulated.*

## FAQ copy

**Q: When should I take Luma Gut?**
A: Take two gummies with your largest meal of the day — typically lunch or dinner. Taking probiotics with food helps protect them through stomach acid and improves the chance they reach the gut alive.

**Q: How long until probiotics actually work?**
A: Most people notice digestive comfort improvements within the first two weeks. The deeper microbiome shifts probiotics support — increased microbial diversity, improved gut barrier — typically take four to six weeks of consistent daily use.

**Q: Do probiotic gummies need refrigeration?**
A: No. Luma Gut uses a shelf-stable probiotic formulation that doesn't require refrigeration. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Stable for 24 months from manufacture.

**Q: Can I take Luma Gut with food allergies?**
A: Luma Gut is gluten-free, dairy-free, soy-free, and vegan. The Citrus Mint flavor uses natural fruit extracts. As always, check the ingredient label and consult a healthcare provider if you have specific food sensitivities.

**Q: Does Luma Gut help with bloating?**
