## Ingredient explainer copy

**Ashwagandha (KSM-66) — 300mg.** The most clinically-studied form of ashwagandha, standardized to 5% withanolides. Research suggests it supports the body's natural cortisol response over time. Adaptogens like ashwagandha work cumulatively — most people notice the deeper effect after four to eight weeks of daily use.

**L-Theanine — 200mg.** A naturally-occurring amino acid found in green tea leaves. L-theanine promotes alpha brain wave activity, which is associated with relaxed alertness. It works within 30-60 minutes and is one of the few ingredients that supports calm without producing drowsiness.

**Lemon Balm — 150mg.** A botanical from the mint family used for centuries to support a sense of calm. Modern research suggests it may help ease everyday tension and support a steady mood.

**Magnesium Glycinate — 100mg.** The chelated form of magnesium most studied for absorption and tolerance. Magnesium plays roles in over 300 biochemical reactions, including those that support nervous system balance.

*No proprietary blends. Every dose is listed exactly as formulated.*

## FAQ copy

**Q: When should I take Luma Calm?**
A: Take two gummies anytime — morning, midday, or early evening. Many customers take them as a midday reset or before a stressful meeting. For best long-term results, take consistently for at least four weeks.

**Q: Will Luma Calm make me drowsy?**
A: No. Luma Calm uses L-theanine (which supports calm without sedation) rather than melatonin or stronger sedatives. You should feel steadier, not sleepier. If you want sleep support specifically, see Luma Sleep.

**Q: How long until I notice a difference?**
A: L-theanine works within 30-60 minutes for in-the-moment calm. Ashwagandha works cumulatively — most people notice the deeper, daily benefit (steadier baseline mood, less reactivity) over four to eight weeks of consistent use.

**Q: Can I take Luma Calm with Luma Energy?**
A: Yes — many customers do. Luma Energy in the morning, Luma Calm midday or before stressful periods. The Energy + Calm Bundle is built for this exact pairing.

**Q: Is Luma Calm safe to take every day?**
A: Yes. The ingredients are formulated for daily use, and consistency is what produces the most meaningful results. Always consult a healthcare provider if you're pregnant, nursing, or on medication.
