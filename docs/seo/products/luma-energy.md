## Ingredient explainer copy (for "Ingredients & doses" accordion)

**Ginseng Root — 200mg.** A traditional adaptogen used for centuries to support physical and mental endurance. Modern research suggests Ginseng helps the body manage daily energy demands without the spike-and-crash pattern of stimulants.

**Vitamin B12 (Methylcobalamin) — 1000mcg.** B12 is essential for red blood cell production and the cellular machinery that turns food into usable energy. Most adults benefit from supplemental B12, especially those eating plant-forward diets. Methylcobalamin is the most bioavailable form.

**Green Tea Extract — 150mg.** Provides a small, natural lift through L-theanine and trace caffeine. Standardized for EGCG, the polyphenol most studied for cellular metabolic support.

**CoQ10 (Ubiquinone) — 50mg.** A naturally-occurring antioxidant that supports mitochondrial function — the cellular "engines" that produce ATP. Levels naturally decline with age, making supplementation increasingly relevant for adults 30+.

*No proprietary blends. Every dose is listed exactly as formulated.*

## FAQ copy (5 Q&A pairs — for FAQPage schema)

**Q: When should I take Luma Energy?**
A: Take two gummies in the morning, anytime before 2pm, with or without food. Most people pair it with their first cup of coffee or tea. Avoid taking later in the day if you're sensitive to even mild stimulating ingredients like green tea extract.

**Q: Does Luma Energy contain caffeine?**
A: Yes — a small natural amount from green tea extract, typically less than 15mg per serving (about one-tenth of a cup of coffee). The energy support primarily comes from non-stimulant ingredients like Ginseng, B12, and CoQ10.

**Q: Will Luma Energy make me jittery?**
A: No. Because we use natural energy support ingredients (not added caffeine, guarana, or synthetic stimulants), most people experience steady energy without jitters or a crash. If you're highly caffeine-sensitive, start with one gummy.

**Q: How long until I notice a difference?**
A: Most people notice steadier morning energy within the first week of consistent daily use. Adaptogens like Ginseng work cumulatively — full effect typically emerges over four to six weeks of daily use.

**Q: Can I take Luma Energy with coffee?**
A: Yes. Many customers do. Luma Energy provides a sustained baseline of energy support that complements (rather than competes with) caffeine's faster, shorter lift. Just keep total daily caffeine intake reasonable — most adults do well with under 400mg.
