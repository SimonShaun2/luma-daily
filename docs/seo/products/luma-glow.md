## Ingredient explainer copy

**Marine Collagen Peptides — 2,500mg.** Hydrolyzed collagen sourced from sustainable wild-caught marine sources. Hydrolyzed peptides have a smaller molecular size than native collagen, making them more bioavailable. Marine collagen is rich in Type I collagen — the dominant structural collagen in skin.

**Vitamin C — 500mg.** Essential for your body's own collagen synthesis — without adequate vitamin C, you can't make collagen well, even with collagen peptide supplementation. We use the buffered ascorbate form for stomach comfort.

**Biotin (Vitamin B7) — 5,000mcg.** Plays a key role in keratin production. Keratin is the structural protein in hair and nails. The dose used here is consistent with most hair, skin, and nail research.

**Hyaluronic Acid — 100mg.** A naturally-occurring molecule that holds 1,000 times its weight in water. Oral hyaluronic acid supports skin moisture from the inside.

*No proprietary blends. Every dose is listed exactly as formulated.*

## FAQ copy

**Q: When should I take Luma Glow?**
A: Take two gummies daily, at any time. Many customers take them with breakfast or as part of an evening routine. Consistency matters more than timing — collagen synthesis is cumulative.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
A: Nails typically show changes first (3-4 weeks) — they're the easiest to track. Hair changes follow (4-8 weeks). Skin elasticity and texture changes are usually most visible at 8-12 weeks of consistent daily use. Take a baseline photo so you have something to compare to.

**Q: Do collagen gummies actually work?**
A: Studies on hydrolyzed collagen peptides suggest they support skin elasticity, hydration, and dermal collagen density when taken consistently at clinically-relevant doses (2,500-5,000mg) for 8-12+ weeks. Luma Glow uses 2,500mg of hydrolyzed marine collagen — within that effective range.

**Q: Should I take Luma Glow with collagen powder too?**
A: It's not necessary. Most people don't need to stack collagen sources. If you do use a separate collagen powder, just check the total daily intake — most adults do well with 5,000-10,000mg total per day across all sources.

**Q: Is Luma Glow vegan?**
A: The marine collagen used in Luma Glow is sustainably-sourced from wild-caught fish, so the formula is not strictly vegan (though it is gluten-free and non-GMO). Plant-based alternatives don't yet match the bioavailability of hydrolyzed marine collagen.
