## Ingredient explainer copy

**Melatonin — 3mg.** A naturally-occurring hormone that signals your body it's time to sleep. Sleep research increasingly favors lower doses (1-3mg) — they work as effectively as 5-10mg doses with significantly less next-day grogginess and a lower chance of disrupting your natural melatonin production over time.

**Chamomile Extract — 200mg.** A traditional botanical used for thousands of years to support a sense of calm. The active compound, apigenin, binds to receptors that support the wind-down transition into sleep.

**Passionflower — 150mg.** A botanical with a long history in supporting the transition to restful sleep. Often paired with chamomile for a fuller wind-down profile.

**GABA — 100mg.** Gamma-Aminobutyric acid, the primary inhibitory neurotransmitter in the human nervous system. Supports the deeper sleep cycles that produce the actual restorative benefit of sleep.

*No proprietary blends. Every dose is listed exactly as formulated.*

## FAQ copy

**Q: When should I take Luma Sleep?**
A: Take two gummies 30-60 minutes before your intended bedtime. Take consistently — your body responds best to predictable timing. Avoid taking earlier in the day or after midnight, as melatonin works by signaling time-of-day to your circadian rhythm.

**Q: Will Luma Sleep make me groggy in the morning?**
A: Most people don't experience next-day grogginess from Luma Sleep — that's specifically why we use 3mg of melatonin instead of the 5-10mg megadoses common in many sleep gummies. If you do feel groggy, try taking just one gummy.

**Q: Is melatonin safe to take every night?**
A: Research suggests low-dose melatonin (1-3mg) is safe for nightly use for most adults. We use 3mg for that reason. Always consult a healthcare provider for long-term use, especially if you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, or under 18.

**Q: What if Luma Sleep doesn't work for me?**
A: If you're still struggling with sleep after consistent use, the issue often isn't the supplement — it's behavior (light exposure, screens, bedtime variability, caffeine timing) or stress. Try pairing with our [nighttime wellness routine guide](/blog/nighttime-wellness-routine), and consider Luma Calm earlier in the evening if stress is the underlying issue.

**Q: Can I take Luma Sleep with Luma Calm?**
A: Yes. A common pattern: Luma Calm in the early evening (5-7pm) for stress balance, Luma Sleep 30-60 minutes before bed. The Sleep + Glow Bundle is built for the full evening routine.
