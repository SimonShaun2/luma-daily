- Habits & Hustle (Jen Cohen)

**Strategy:** 1 podcast pitch per week, founder-led. Aim for 1 booking per month at tier-2, 1 per quarter at tier-1.

## Tier 5 — Creator + influencer

Creators who naturally cover wellness, beauty, or routine content.

### Macro (>500K followers) — paid + relationship
- Mari Llewellyn, Whitney Simmons, Kelly Leveque, Sahara Rose

### Mid (50K-500K followers) — gifting + light paid
- Wellness creators on Instagram + TikTok with consistent supplement / routine content

### Micro (10K-50K followers) — gifting only
- Targeted gifting program: 50-100 carefully-selected creators per quarter

**Strategy:** Avoid buying #ad placements at this stage — they don't generate sustainable backlinks. Instead, gifting + organic mentions + affiliate program.

## Tier 6 — HARO + Qwoted journalist requests

The single highest-volume tactic. Sign up for:
- **HARO** (Help A Reporter Out) — 3 emails/day, scan for wellness, supplements, founder advice
- **Qwoted** — similar, often higher quality
- **Featured.com** — newer, premium tier
- **SourceBottle** — Australian + global

**Strategy:** 5 responses per week. 2-3 yield citations per month after first 30 days. Founder responds personally — never use a generic template.

---

## Tier 7 — Founder-owned channels

Long-game. Builds founder brand entity authority, generates inbound press inquiries.

- **LinkedIn** — 1 post/week, focus on wellness category insights, never sell
- **Substack** — 1 longer essay/month, "behind the scenes of building a wellness brand"
- **Medium / personal blog** — repurpose Substack
- **Twitter/X** — wellness commentary, light cadence

## Outreach contact discovery

For each target, find the right person:

1. **Author byline check** — who wrote similar pieces in the last 12 months? They're the right contact.
2. **Twitter / LinkedIn** — many editors are reachable directly there
3. **Hunter.io / Apollo** — for verified email when needed
4. **PressFarm / MuckRack** — premium tools with editor databases

**Don't use generic info@ or editor@ addresses.** Personal outreach to a named editor has 5-10x the response rate of generic addresses.

---

## Tracking

Maintain a CRM (Notion, Airtable, or HubSpot Free) with one row per outreach contact:
- Name, outlet, role, email, LinkedIn
- First contact date, response, follow-up dates
- Pitch angle used
- Outcome (placed, declined, no response)
- Last touched

Aim for ~50 active contacts in the CRM after 60 days.
