# Email Outreach Templates

Templates calibrated by audience type. All keep three rules:
1. Subject line under 50 chars, specific, no clickbait
2. First line shows research (reference recent work)
3. Clear ask in 2 sentences max

---

**Tactical notes:**

- Always provide bio + headshot + logo links upfront
- Always mention deadline-awareness
- Never use generic "we are a leading provider" language

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Initial pitch |
| Day 5 | Soft follow-up: "Wanted to bump this — happy to provide more info or different angle" |
| Day 12 | Final follow-up: "Last note — totally fine if not a fit. Will hold off after this." |
| Never | Past 12 days, drop them. Re-engage in 90 days with a different angle if relevant. |

Don't be the founder who emails 7 times. The 3-touch cadence converts better than longer sequences.
