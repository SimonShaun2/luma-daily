# 90-Day Outreach Calendar

Week-by-week schedule for the first 90 days of backlink + PR outreach. Designed for one full-time outreach lead + ~4 hours/week of founder time.

---

## Days 1-14 — Setup wave

### Week 1
- [ ] Set up CRM (Notion / Airtable / HubSpot Free)
- [ ] Sign up for HARO, Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle
- [ ] Build target list: 50 wellness publishers + 30 podcasts + 100 creators
- [ ] Create press kit page at takelumadaily.com/press (logos, founder bio, product photos, fact sheet)
- [ ] Founder LinkedIn audit + 3 founder posts published
- [ ] Set up brand monitoring (Google Alerts, Brand24 free trial)

### Week 2
- [ ] First 3 HARO responses sent
- [ ] First wellness publisher pitch (Tier 2: mindbodygreen, Well+Good, Byrdie — pick one)
- [ ] First podcast pitch (Tier 3 niche show — high booking probability)
- [ ] Creator gifting list finalized (25 micro-influencers for first wave)
- [ ] Founder LinkedIn: 3 more posts

---

## Days 15-30 — Volume wave

### Week 3
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] 2 wellness publisher pitches
- [ ] 1 podcast pitch
- [ ] First 25 creator gifting boxes shipped
- [ ] First Reddit AMA / Q&A response in r/Supplements (no link — establish founder voice first)
- [ ] Founder Substack launched + first essay published
- [ ] LinkedIn: 3 founder posts

### Week 4
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] 2 wellness publisher follow-ups
- [ ] 2 new podcast pitches
- [ ] First 5 directory listings live (Crunchbase, LinkedIn, Trustpilot, BBB, GBP)
- [ ] Founder podcast pre-recorded (first booking from week 2-3 pitches lands here)
- [ ] LinkedIn: 3 founder posts

**Day 30 review:**
- HARO citations placed (target: 2)
- Referring domains acquired (target: 15)
- Podcast bookings (target: 2)
- Founder LinkedIn followers (target: 250)

---

## Days 31-60 — Editorial push wave

### Week 5
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] First Tier-1 publisher pitch (NYT, Vogue, Glamour, Real Simple — pick one)
- [ ] 3 podcast pitches
- [ ] First wellness publisher feature placed (typically lands here from week 2-4 pitches)
- [ ] Founder Substack: second essay
- [ ] Second wave of creator gifting (25 more)

### Week 6
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] 2 follow-ups on outstanding pitches
- [ ] 2 new podcast pitches
- [ ] First press release published: "Luma Daily launches Find My Ritual quiz"
- [ ] PR distribution: 5 niche outlets via direct contact (skip wire services — low ROI)
- [ ] First gift guide submission (start Q4 push)

### Week 7
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] First creator collab launched (paid micro-influencer, $500-$1500 range)
- [ ] 2 podcast appearances (recordings + live releases)
- [ ] LinkedIn cadence: 3 posts
- [ ] First "best wellness gummies of 2026" roundup pitch

### Week 8
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] First Tier-1 publisher follow-up + alternative angle
- [ ] 2 podcast pitches
- [ ] Founder Q&A / AMA on Reddit (now appropriate after 6+ weeks of organic Reddit presence)
- [ ] Affiliate program soft-launch to top 10 wellness bloggers

**Day 60 review:**
- HARO citations placed (target: 6 cumulative)
- Referring domains (target: 35)
- Podcast appearances (target: 4 cumulative)
- Editorial features (target: 1+)
- LinkedIn followers (target: 600)

---

## Days 61-90 — Compound wave

### Week 9
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] 2 follow-ups on Tier-1 outstanding pitches
- [ ] 2 podcast pitches
- [ ] First "best wellness gummies" roundup placement (typically lands here from earlier pitches)
- [ ] Affiliate program full launch — 25 invited bloggers/creators
- [ ] Founder LinkedIn: increase to 5 posts/week

### Week 10
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] Pitch Tier-1 holiday gift guide (window closes ~September 15 for major print outlets)
- [ ] 2 podcast appearances (recorded earlier + released)
- [ ] First long-form Substack guest essay placed on a Tier-2 wellness Substack
- [ ] First Vogue / NYT / Bon Appetit pitch (founder profile angle)

### Week 11
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] 3 podcast pitches
- [ ] Reddit Q&A doubled — now appropriate to occasionally drop URL
- [ ] First wellness category awards submission (Best DTC Brand 2026, Newcomer Brand of the Year)

### Week 12
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] Year-in-review founder essay published on LinkedIn + repurposed to Substack
- [ ] First major influencer collab launched ($5K-$10K range, mid-tier creator)
- [ ] Pitch retrospective: which channels worked, which didn't, calendar for next 90 days

### Week 13
- [ ] 5 HARO responses
- [ ] Holiday gift guide submissions complete
- [ ] Founder podcast: 1-2 final tier-2 bookings
- [ ] Trustpilot review milestone push (target: 100 verified reviews by day 90)
- [ ] Comprehensive 90-day review

**Day 90 review:**
- HARO citations placed (target: 12+)
- Referring domains (target: 75+)
- DR 60+ referring domains (target: 10+)
- Podcast appearances (target: 5+)
- Editorial features (target: 3+)
- Founder LinkedIn followers (target: 1500+)
- Branded search volume increase (target: +200%)
- AI Overview citation rate (target: 20% on tracked queries)
