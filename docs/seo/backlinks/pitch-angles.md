# Pitch Angles — Luma Daily

The story angles that make journalists, podcast hosts, and bloggers actually want to write about / interview Luma Daily. Each angle is calibrated for a specific audience type.

The principle: never pitch your product. Pitch a story your product happens to be relevant to.

---

## Angle 1 — *"The daily gummy ritual replacing the random supplement cabinet"*

**Audience:** Wellness publishers (Well+Good, mindbodygreen, The Cut), trend pieces.

**Hook:** A category shift happening in 2026 — buyers are abandoning the 6-bottle "stack of the month" pattern in favor of curated, ritual-based wellness systems.

**Why it lands:** Trend pieces want a category shift to write about, not a product to feature. This positions Luma Daily as evidence of the trend, not the trend itself.

**Quote-worthy data points:**
- The average wellness consumer buys 11 supplements per year and abandons 70% of them within 30 days
- "Wellness routine" search volume up 340% over 3 years vs "best supplement" up only 12%
- The rise of ritual-based wellness brands (Lemme, Ritual, Luma Daily) tracks the broader cultural shift toward consistency over intensity

**Pitch one-liner:** "I've been tracking a clear shift in wellness purchasing behavior — buyers are moving from random supplement buying to curated daily rituals. Would love to share the data and one founder's perspective if it'd be useful."

## Angle 2 — *"A premium wellness gummy brand built around routines, not single SKUs"*

**Audience:** DTC + business publications (Vogue Business, Glossy, Modern Retail).

**Hook:** Luma Daily is a category response — five formulas designed as a system rather than five separate products. Different brand-building approach than Olly (single-SKU) or Ritual (multivitamin).

**Why it lands:** Business publications love DTC strategy stories. This is a clear positioning + brand-building case study.

**Pitch one-liner:** "I'm the founder of Luma Daily, a 2026-launched wellness brand built around a five-formula daily ritual instead of single-SKU supplements. Happy to share the strategy thinking + early results if it'd make a story for [outlet]."

---

## Angle 3 — *"How Luma Daily helps simplify supplement consistency"*

**Audience:** Practical wellness blogs (Wellness Mama, Apartment Therapy), routine-focused content.

**Hook:** Most people fail at supplement consistency. The fix is product + system + routine design — not motivation.

**Why it lands:** Practical wellness audiences love systems-over-willpower content. Pairs with founder expertise.

**Quote-worthy data points:**
- 70% of supplement buyers quit within 30 days
- The #1 reason: complexity + reorder friction
- The fix: anchor habits + pre-built systems + automated reorder

**Pitch one-liner:** "Wanted to flag a piece I think your audience would resonate with — practical fixes for supplement consistency, written from the perspective of a brand built around solving exactly that. Happy to contribute as a guest essay or as expert quotes for an existing article."

---

## Angle 4 — *Founder personal story*

**Audience:** Podcast hosts (Almost 30, Skinny Confidential, Habits & Hustle), profile-style features.

**Hook:** Whatever the founder's actual personal "why" is for building Luma Daily. Genuine, specific, ideally tied to a moment of insight or frustration.

**Template:** "I built Luma Daily after [specific moment/frustration]. The wellness industry was [observation]. So we [response]."

**Why it lands:** Podcast hosts and profile features want the human story, not the product pitch.

**Pitch one-liner:** "Reaching out as a wellness founder with what I think is a genuinely interesting category-shift story — I'd love to come on the show and share how Luma Daily came together (the brand-building, the founder side, the lessons learned)."

---

## Angle 5 — *Ingredient + science deep dives*

**Audience:** Wellness/nutrition publishers, ingredient-focused content (Healthline, EatingWell, Examine.com).

**Hook:** Founder-as-expert quotes on specific ingredients and routine design.

**Sub-angles:**
- Why low-dose melatonin (3mg) beats high-dose (10mg)
- The case for KSM-66 ashwagandha specifically
- Why morning ritual + night ritual matters more than any single supplement
- Marine vs bovine vs vegan collagen — the practical guide

**Why it lands:** Publishers always need expert quotes. Founder-as-expert is sustainable inbound source over time.

**Pitch one-liner:** "I'm the founder of Luma Daily — happy to be a source on wellness gummy and supplement routine topics. If you cover [topic], here's a take that might be useful: [60-word expert quote]."

---

## Angle 6 — *Year-end / seasonal gift guides*

**Audience:** All major lifestyle outlets in Q4 (Vogue, Glamour, Real Simple, NYT Wirecutter).

**Hook:** "The wellness gift that's actually used past January"

**Why it lands:** Holiday gift guides are the highest-traffic content these outlets publish, and they always include a wellness category. Position Luma Daily as the considered wellness gift.

**Pitch timing:** Q4 gift guide pitches must go out August 1-September 15. Earlier than feels reasonable.

**Pitch one-liner:** "Pitching for [outlet]'s 2026 holiday wellness gift guide. Luma Daily is a premium wellness gummy brand built around the daily ritual concept — included details below."

## Angle 7 — *Roundup positioning ("best wellness gummies")*

**Audience:** Anyone publishing a category roundup (The Strategist, NYT Wirecutter, Byrdie, Healthline).

**Hook:** Submit Luma Daily for inclusion when these publishers run their annual / quarterly category roundups.

**Why it lands:** Roundup placements = both ranking authority AND brand awareness AND direct traffic. The trifecta.

**Pitch timing:** Monitor each publisher's editorial calendar. Reach out 3-4 weeks before publication.

**Pitch one-liner:** "Submitting Luma Daily for consideration in your upcoming wellness gummy roundup. Differentiation: five-formula daily ritual system, transparent dosing, third-party tested. Detailed product info below."

---

## Common patterns across all pitches

✅ **Lead with story value, not product.**
✅ **Specific data points beat generic claims.**
✅ **Founder voice + first-person pronoun.**
✅ **Reference the writer's recent work.** ("Loved your piece on X — wanted to share something I thought might fit your beat.")
✅ **Make the next step clear.** ("Happy to share more / get on a 15-min call / send product / put you in touch with the founder.")

❌ **Avoid:** generic "we make great supplements," "synergy," "revolutionary," "game-changing," PR-speak in any form.
