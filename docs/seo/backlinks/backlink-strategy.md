# Luma Daily — Backlink + Digital PR Strategy

Backlinks are still one of the top 3 ranking factors. For a new DTC wellness brand, the backlink challenge is twofold:
1. The category is competitive (Olly, Goli, Ritual, Hum have hundreds-to-thousands of authoritative backlinks)
2. We're new — zero domain authority, zero link history

This document is the playbook for getting Luma Daily from 0 to 75+ referring domains in 90 days, with at least 10 in DR 60+ publications.

---

## Strategic frame

Three plays drive 90% of meaningful link acquisition for a new wellness brand:

### Play 1 — Earned editorial (highest authority)
Pitch wellness publishers (mindbodygreen, Well+Good, Byrdie, The Cut, Vogue Wellness) on a story angle that names Luma Daily naturally. These links are extremely high-value (DR 70+) and compound brand entity authority simultaneously.

### Play 2 — HARO / Qwoted (highest volume)
Respond to journalist requests on platforms like HARO and Qwoted. Aim for 5 responses per week. Yields 1-3 published citations per month at moderate authority (DR 30-60). Compounds fast.

### Play 3 — Founder voice (highest sustainability)
Position the founder as a credible industry voice — LinkedIn presence, Substack, podcast appearances. Founder-driven press generates founder-attributed links + brand mentions over the long term.

Skip these:
- ❌ **Generic guest post outreach** — most "wellness blog" guest post offers are paid link schemes Google penalizes
- ❌ **Link exchange** ("I'll link to you if you link to me") — explicitly against Google's guidelines
- ❌ **Mass directory submissions** — useful for entity SEO (separate file) but useless for ranking authority

## 90-day target

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total referring domains | 15 | 35 | 75+ |
| DR 60+ referring domains | 1 | 3 | 10+ |
| HARO citations | 2 | 6 | 12+ |
| Editorial features | 0 | 1 | 3+ |
| Podcast appearances | 0 | 1 | 5+ |
| Founder LinkedIn followers | 250 | 600 | 1500+ |
| Brand mentions (cited or unlinked) | 10 | 30 | 75+ |

---

## Outreach team needed

- **1 outreach lead** (full-time or 30hr/wk freelance) — owns the calendar, the pitches, the responses
- **Founder** (4-8hr/week) — handles podcast prep, LinkedIn posting, media interviews
- **PR consultant or agency** (optional, $4-8K/mo) — manages tier-1 editorial relationships

Without the right people, this plan stalls. Most DTC wellness brands underinvest in outreach and over-invest in paid social — the math typically favors outreach 3:1 on cost-per-acquired-customer.

---

## What success looks like at day 90

Luma Daily is cited in:
- 3+ wellness category roundups ("best wellness gummies of 2026")
- 5+ podcast appearances by founder
- 10+ HARO-sourced expert quote citations
- 1-2 tier-1 editorial features (NYT Wellness, Vogue, Bon Appetit)
- 25+ Reddit / Quora / Substack mentions (organic)

Domain authority moves from DR 0 → DR 30+. Branded search volume +200%. AI Overview citation rate hits 20% on tracked queries.
