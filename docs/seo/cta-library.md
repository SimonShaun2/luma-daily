## Voice + tone reference

**Tone of CTAs:**
- Action verb first ("Build", "Get", "Add", "Take", "Subscribe")
- Personal pronouns where they feel natural ("My Ritual", "My Stack")
- No exclamation marks
- No ALL CAPS in body copy *(uppercase OK in button copy when used consistently — e.g., "BUILD MY RITUAL")*
- Outcome over feature ("Sleep Deeper" > "Buy Sleep Gummies")

**Length:**
- Hero CTAs: 2–4 words
- Mid-page CTAs: up to 6 words
- Email subject CTAs: 4–7 words
- Footer / utility CTAs: 1–3 words

**Color / visual:**
- Primary CTA: solid amber `#C8813A` background, white text, rounded-full, all-caps
- Secondary CTA: outline (charcoal or cream depending on background), title-case
- Tertiary CTA: text link with arrow

## Compliance check

All 75 CTAs use action-language only — no health-claim language. None imply curing, treating, preventing, or diagnosing any condition.

Compliant phrases used:
- "Build my ritual" / "Build my stack" / "Build my routine"
- "Find my formulas" / "Find what fits"
- "Subscribe & Save" / "Save more when you bundle"
- "Sleep deeper" *(structure/function — describes outcome, doesn't claim cure)*

Avoided:
- "Fix your sleep" / "Cure your stress"
- "Eliminate fatigue" / "End anxiety"
- Any disease-name CTA
