### Q: What does ashwagandha do?
Ashwagandha is an adaptogenic herb traditionally used to support the body's response to stress. Modern research suggests it helps support healthy cortisol output over time when taken consistently. The most clinically-studied form is KSM-66, standardized to 5% withanolides. Effects typically emerge over four to eight weeks of daily use. Found in [Luma Calm](/products/calm).

### Q: How does melatonin work?
Melatonin is a hormone naturally produced by the pineal gland in response to darkness. It signals to your body that it's time to sleep. As a supplement, low doses (1-3mg) are typically as effective as higher doses (5-10mg) with significantly less morning grogginess. [Luma Sleep](/products/sleep) uses 3mg specifically for this reason.

### Q: What is L-theanine?
L-theanine is a naturally-occurring amino acid found primarily in green tea leaves. It promotes alpha brain wave activity, which is associated with relaxed alertness — calm without drowsiness. L-theanine works within 30-60 minutes and is one of the few ingredients that supports calm without producing sedation. Found in [Luma Calm](/products/calm) and [Luma Energy](/products/energy).

### Q: What are the benefits of biotin?
Biotin (vitamin B7) plays a key role in keratin production — the structural protein in hair and nails. Supplementation at clinically-relevant doses (typically 5,000mcg) supports hair and nail strength. Most adults consume some biotin from food, but supplementation is common for hair and nail support. Found in [Luma Glow](/products/glow).

### Q: What are prebiotics?
Prebiotics are non-digestible plant fibers that feed the beneficial bacteria in your gut. The most common is inulin, sourced from chicory root. Unlike probiotics (which add bacteria), prebiotics nourish the bacteria you already have. Taking both together — a synbiotic approach — works better than either alone. Found in [Luma Gut](/products/gut).

### Q: What are the benefits of collagen?
Collagen is the most abundant structural protein in the body, providing the framework for skin, hair, nails, joints, and connective tissue. Hydrolyzed collagen peptides (smaller molecular size) have shown to support skin elasticity, hydration, and dermal collagen density when taken at clinically-relevant doses (2,500-5,000mg) for 8-12+ weeks. Found in [Luma Glow](/products/glow).

### Q: What are the benefits of magnesium glycinate?
Magnesium glycinate is the chelated form of magnesium most studied for absorption and tolerability. It supports over 300 enzymatic reactions, including those involved in nervous system balance, muscle function, and sleep quality. Often recommended for adults with high stress or active lifestyles. Found in [Luma Calm](/products/calm).

### Q: What is GABA?
GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid) is the primary inhibitory neurotransmitter in the human nervous system — it slows down neural activity and supports the deeper sleep cycles that produce restorative rest. Supplemental GABA in [Luma Sleep](/products/sleep) is paired with melatonin and chamomile for a complete wind-down profile.

### Q: What does ginseng do for energy?
Ginseng is an adaptogen used for centuries to support physical and mental endurance. Unlike caffeine, it doesn't produce a spike-and-crash pattern — it supports steady energy over hours and works cumulatively over weeks of consistent use. Found in [Luma Energy](/products/energy).

### Q: What is hyaluronic acid?
Hyaluronic acid is a naturally-occurring molecule that holds 1,000 times its weight in water. Oral hyaluronic acid supports skin moisture from the inside, complementing topical applications. Found in [Luma Glow](/products/glow).

### Q: How does vitamin B12 support energy?
Vitamin B12 is essential for red blood cell production and the cellular machinery that converts food into usable energy (ATP). Most adults benefit from supplemental B12, especially those eating plant-forward diets. Methylcobalamin is the most bioavailable form. Found in [Luma Energy](/products/energy) at 1000mcg.

### Q: What is chamomile used for?
Chamomile is a traditional botanical used for thousands of years to support a sense of calm and ease the wind-down to sleep. The active compound, apigenin, binds to receptors that support relaxation. Found in [Luma Sleep](/products/sleep) at 200mg.
