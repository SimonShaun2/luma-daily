# FAQ — Shipping + Returns

7 Q&As for the shipping/returns/contact pages. Implement with `FAQPage` schema.

---

### Q: How long does Luma Daily shipping take?
Standard shipping is 3-5 business days within the continental US. Expedited (2-day) shipping is available at checkout. International shipping is currently to Canada (5-7 business days) and the UK (7-10 business days).

### Q: Is shipping free?
Free shipping on all subscription orders, regardless of size. One-time orders ship free over $60. Standard shipping on smaller one-time orders is $4.99.

### Q: Where do you ship?
Currently the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. We're expanding to additional countries in 2026 — sign up for our newsletter to get notified when your country becomes available.

### Q: How do I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking email within 24 hours of your order shipping. You can also see all orders and tracking links in your account portal.

### Q: What's your return policy?
60-day money-back guarantee on your first order. If you're not satisfied with the formula for any reason, contact customer support within 60 days of delivery for a full refund — no need to return the product. Subsequent orders are eligible for return within 30 days if unopened.

### Q: My order was damaged in shipping. What now?
Email customer support with a photo of the damage. We'll send a free replacement immediately, no return needed.

### Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact customer support and we can update the address. Once an order has shipped, we can't intercept it — though USPS / FedEx may allow address corrections through their own systems.
