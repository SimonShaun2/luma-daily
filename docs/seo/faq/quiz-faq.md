# FAQ — Find My Ritual Quiz

5 Q&As designed for the quiz page. Implement with `FAQPage` schema. Quiz is the highest-conversion page on the site — the FAQ here addresses the friction that prevents quiz starts ("how long does it take," "what does it ask," "is it accurate").

---

### Q: How does the Find My Ritual quiz work?
The Find My Ritual quiz is four short questions that take about 60 seconds to complete. It asks about your primary wellness goal, when you typically take supplements, secondary areas you'd like to support, and your current routine. Based on your answers, it recommends the two-to-three Luma Daily formulas that best fit your goals — plus a personalized timing schedule and an option to subscribe.

### Q: How accurate is the quiz?
The quiz uses the same logic our wellness team uses to recommend formulas to customers in person. It's not a clinical assessment — it's a personalized recommendation based on goals and lifestyle. If you have specific health concerns or are on medication, consult a healthcare provider before adding new supplements.

### Q: Do I have to enter my email?
No. The quiz works without an email — you can see your results and add formulas to your cart immediately. Entering your email at the end gets you a 15% discount code for your first order, plus your saved ritual recommendations.

### Q: Can I retake the quiz later?
Yes — anytime. Goals change, routines change, the formulas that fit you can change too. Many customers retake the quiz seasonally or when their schedule shifts.

### Q: What if the quiz recommends formulas I'm not interested in?
You can adjust the recommendation directly on the results page — swap formulas, remove ones you don't want, add others that interest you. The quiz is a starting point, not a final answer.
