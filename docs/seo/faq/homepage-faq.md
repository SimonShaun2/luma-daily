Subscribe to any formula or bundle and save 20% on every order, with free shipping. Choose your delivery frequency (typically every 30 days). ### Q: When should I take Luma Daily gummies?
Take Luma Energy in the morning. Luma Calm any time you need stress balance — most people take it midday or before stressful periods. Luma Sleep 30-60 minutes before bed. Luma Glow and Luma Gut can be taken any time of day, ideally with food. The [Find My Ritual quiz](/quiz) builds a personalized timing schedule.

### Q: How long until I notice results?
It varies by formula. Luma Sleep and Luma Calm have the fastest noticeable effects (within the first night or first 30-60 minutes). Adaptogens like ashwagandha (in Luma Calm) and ingredients like collagen (in Luma Glow) work cumulatively — full effect typically emerges over 4-12 weeks of consistent daily use. Consistency matters more than dose for most ingredients.
