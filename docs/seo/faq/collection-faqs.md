## Gut Health FAQ

### Q: What's the difference between prebiotics and probiotics?
Probiotics are the live beneficial bacteria. Prebiotics are the fibers (like inulin from chicory root) that feed those bacteria. Taking both together — a synbiotic approach — works better than either alone for daily gut wellness. Luma Gut combines both in one formula.

### Q: Do gut health gummies need refrigeration?
Luma Gut doesn't require refrigeration. We use a shelf-stable probiotic formulation that maintains potency at room temperature for 24 months from manufacture.

### Q: How long until probiotics work?
Most people notice digestive comfort improvements within the first two weeks. Deeper microbiome shifts typically establish over four to six weeks of consistent daily use.
