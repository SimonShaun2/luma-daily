# FAQ — Subscriptions

7 Q&As for the subscription / account pages. Address every common subscription friction point upfront — every question a customer doesn't have to email customer service about is a friction point removed from the subscribe decision. Implement with `FAQPage` schema.

---

### Q: How does the Luma Daily subscription work?
Subscribe to any formula or bundle and your order ships automatically on the schedule you choose (default: every 30 days). You save 20% on every order, get free shipping always, and can pause, skip, or cancel anytime through your account portal. There's no minimum commitment and no fees.

### Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the account portal in under 30 seconds — no fees, no questions, no minimum commitment. You can also pause for any length of time if you just need a break.

### Q: How do I pause or skip a subscription order?
Log into your account, click "Subscriptions," and you'll see options to pause, skip the next shipment, or change your delivery frequency. All changes take effect immediately and apply to future orders only.

### Q: When am I charged for my subscription?
You're charged when each order ships. We send a reminder email 3 days before each order so you have time to skip, change, or cancel before the charge.

### Q: Can I change which products are in my subscription?
Yes. Add formulas, remove formulas, swap them out — all from the account portal. Changes apply to your next scheduled order.

### Q: Does the 20% subscription discount apply to bundles?
Yes. Bundles get the 20% subscription discount on top of their bundle pricing. The Daily Ritual Bundle, for example, is $139.99 retail or $111.99 on subscription.

### Q: What if I want to cancel but try one more month?
That's exactly what the "skip next order" feature is for — pause without committing to cancellation. If after the skip you still want to cancel, do it then. Customers often skip once or twice and stay subscribed.
