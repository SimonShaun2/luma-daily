# Luma Daily — Master SEO Strategy

**Domain:** takelumadaily.com
**Brand:** Luma Daily (Luma Wellness Group)
**Positioning:** A daily wellness ritual, not just supplements.
**Primary tagline:** Daily gummies for the way you actually live.

---

## 1. Strategic frame

Luma Daily competes in a crowded, commoditized gummy supplement category dominated by Olly, Goli, Ritual, Lemme, Nature Made, and Hum. Three things separate us:

1. **Ritual-as-product.** We sell *the routine* — a stack of 5 daily gummies, taken in a specific cadence — not single SKUs. Search demand for "wellness routine" and "supplement stack" is rising faster than for individual ingredients.
2. **Editorial wellness brand.** Premium tone, calm visual language, no neon-green packaging or "BOOST!" copy. This earns trust with the 28–45 wellness-curious buyer who has graduated from Goli but doesn't want a $90/month Ritual subscription.
3. **Five formulas, one system.** Energy / Calm / Sleep / Glow / Gut — covers ~80% of daily wellness intent. Bundles + the Find My Ritual quiz convert browsers into multi-SKU buyers.

The SEO job is to own the **"daily wellness ritual"** category before competitors notice it as a category.

## 2. Three pillars

### Pillar A — Category capture (own the ritual)
Rank #1 for the long tail around routines, stacks, rituals. Lower-volume terms but extremely high intent + low competition. This is our white space.

Anchor terms:
- daily wellness routine
- daily wellness gummies
- supplement routine
- wellness ritual
- supplement stack
- gummy vitamins for adults

### Pillar B — Product-category capture
Rank top-3 for the five product categories. Higher volume, higher competition, but every product page must compete here or the funnel breaks.

Anchor terms per SKU:
- energy gummies / energy gummies for adults / natural energy gummies
- calm gummies / stress gummies / ashwagandha gummies
- sleep gummies / melatonin gummies / sleep aid gummies
- collagen gummies / beauty gummies / hair skin nails gummies
- probiotic gummies / gut health gummies

### Pillar C — Educational authority (top of funnel)
Pre-purchase research queries — "how to," "what is," "vs," "best time to." These build topical authority that Google rewards across the whole site, and they feed AI Overviews / ChatGPT product mentions.

Anchor patterns:
- how to build a wellness routine
- magnesium vs melatonin
- prebiotic vs probiotic
- best time to take [X]
- energy gummies vs caffeine

## 7. AI search visibility strategy

LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) increasingly answer wellness questions directly. Optimizing for AI surfaces is no longer optional.

**Tactics:**

1. **FAQ-first content structure.** Every blog post and PDP includes a structured Q&A section using `FAQPage` schema. AI Overviews disproportionately cite FAQ-formatted answers.
2. **Definitional clarity.** First paragraph of every educational post answers the query in 40–60 words, plain English. This is the "AI citation slot" — what the LLM lifts verbatim.
3. **Topical entity completeness.** For every cluster (e.g. "energy gummies"), we cover: what it is, how it works, ingredients, who it's for, when to take, side effects, comparison alternatives. Completeness signals authority to both Google and LLMs.
4. **Citable claims.** Use specific numbers, doses, study references where possible. "Contains 200mg L-theanine" beats "supports calm." LLMs cite specifics.
5. **Author + brand entity building.** Add `Person` and `Organization` schema. Get the brand mentioned on Wikipedia-adjacent sources (Wirecutter, Healthline, Byrdie). LLMs trust entity-cohesive brands.
6. **Public Q&A presence.** Reddit (r/Supplements, r/Nootropics, r/skincareaddiction), Quora, ProductHunt. ChatGPT and Perplexity heavily cite Reddit threads.

**KPI:** track brand mentions in AI Overviews + ChatGPT-cited domains via tools like `peec.ai`, `otterly.ai`, or manual sampling. Target: cited in 20% of relevant AI-answered queries by day 90.

## 8. Internal linking strategy

Detailed in [internal-linking-plan.md](internal-linking-plan.md). Three rules:

1. **Every blog post links to:** the relevant PDP, the Find My Ritual quiz, the relevant bundle, and 2–3 related articles within the same cluster.
2. **Every PDP links to:** related products in the same use-case, the relevant bundle, the educational pillar for that category, and the FAQ.
3. **Pillar pages link down to:** all 8 supporting articles in their cluster, and laterally to other pillar pages.

The architecture is hub-and-spoke: cluster pillars sit at the hubs, each linked from the homepage, navigation, and the supporting spokes.

## 9. Compliance — language guardrails

Luma Daily is a dietary supplement under FDA structure/function rules. We may say products **support** a function. We may **not** claim to cure, treat, prevent, or diagnose any disease.

**Approved phrases:**
- supports energy + focus
- supports calm + stress balance
- supports restful sleep
- supports healthy skin, hair + nails
- supports digestive wellness

**Prohibited terms across all SEO content:**
- anxiety, depression, insomnia, ADHD, IBS, GERD, eczema, acne, etc. (named conditions)
- cure, treat, prevent, diagnose, heal
- "clinically proven to" (unless we have the actual clinical trial)
- "doctor recommended" (unless documented)
- "FDA approved" (supplements are not FDA-approved)

Every page must include the FDA disclaimer where benefits are described:
> *These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.*
