## Section 1 — Why we built Luma Daily

**Headline:** Why we built Luma Daily.

**Body (~150 words):**
The wellness industry has a consistency problem. Most people who buy a supplement quit within 30 days. Most "stacks" are six bottles deep, taken erratically, abandoned by month two. The category responded by selling more aggressive single-SKUs — bigger doses, louder claims, hype-driven packaging.

We went the other direction. Luma Daily started with a simple question: what would a wellness brand look like if it were built around the routine, not the supplement?

The answer is five clean formulas — Energy, Calm, Sleep, Glow, and Gut — designed to fit a real morning-to-night ritual. Not five separate brands. One system, with a quiz that recommends the right two-to-three formulas for each customer's goals and schedule. A subscription that removes the consistency problem. A premium-but-restrained voice that respects the people who buy it.

The result is the daily ritual we wanted to take ourselves.

## Section 2 — Our standards

**Headline:** Our standards, in plain language.

**Body (~100 words):**
- **Third-party tested every batch.** Every Luma Daily formula is tested by an independent ISO-certified lab for ingredient identity, potency, and contaminants. Certificates of Analysis available on request.
- **Transparent dosing.** No proprietary blends. Every ingredient listed with the exact dose, exactly as formulated.
- **Clinically-relevant amounts.** We use the doses research supports — 300mg KSM-66 ashwagandha, 3mg melatonin, 2,500mg marine collagen — not trace amounts that look good on a label.
- **Clean inputs.** Vegan-friendly (with the exception of marine collagen in Glow), gluten-free, non-GMO, no artificial colors, no synthetic dyes.
- **Made in the USA.** Manufactured in cGMP-certified facilities following FDA dietary supplement guidelines.
