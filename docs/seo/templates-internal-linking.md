# Luma Daily SEO Templates + Internal Linking

## Created

- Article-style route: `/blog/:slug`
- Goal landing route: `/goals/:slug`
- Breadcrumb component for articles and goal pages
- Inline quiz CTA component
- Explore by Goal component
- Related products component
- Related articles component

## Useful Landing Pages

These pages are intended as helpful conversion-focused guides, not doorway pages:

- `/goals/energy-gummies`
- `/goals/sleep-gummies`
- `/goals/beauty-gummies`
- `/goals/gut-health-gummies`
- `/goals/daily-wellness-gummies`

Each page includes:

- A clear customer goal
- Practical ritual guidance
- Related products
- FAQ support
- CTA back to the quiz and shop

## Internal Linking Rules Implemented

- Homepage links into Explore by Goal pages.
- Shop page links into Explore by Goal and quiz CTA.
- Product pages link to ritual guide articles.
- Articles link to related products and related articles.
- Goal pages link to products, quiz, and shop.
