## Voice + word-bank reference

**Words we use:**
ritual, daily, calm, considered, clean, real, honest, premium, supports, fits, system, formula, routine, restorative, balance, steady, gentle, quiet, complete, foundation, deeper, smoother

**Words we never use:**
miracle, breakthrough, revolutionary, ultimate, supercharged, super, blast, boost, shred, jacked, ultra, max, extreme, scientifically proven (unless it is), doctor approved (unless it is), guaranteed, cures, treats, prevents, fixes

**Tone modulation:**
- Hero / homepage: calm + aspirational
- PDP subhead: outcome + sensory ("sleep deeper, wake better")
- Email subject lines: practical + warm ("your morning ritual, simplified")
- Paid social: short + benefit-led
- Pack copy: respectful of literacy + scannable

---

## Compliance check

Every tagline above uses **structure/function language** (supports, helps, daily ritual). None of them claim to **cure / treat / prevent / diagnose** any condition.

Banned words checked against this list and confirmed absent:
- anxiety, depression, insomnia, ADHD, IBS, GERD, eczema, acne
- cure, treat, prevent, diagnose, heal
- "FDA approved" (supplements aren't)
- "clinically proven" (not without our own clinical data)
