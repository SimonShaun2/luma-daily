# Luma Daily — Search Intent Map

Every keyword fits a buyer mindset. Targeting the wrong intent — even with great content — is the #1 reason SEO doesn't convert. This document maps the keyword universe to five intent buckets, with the matching content format and conversion path for each.

---

## 1. Transactional intent

**Mindset:** "I'm ready to buy. Show me the option."
**Content format:** Product page, bundle page, cart-direct landing.
**Conversion path:** PDP → cart → checkout. Skip education.
**On-page priority:** price, "Add to Cart", trust badges, social proof, fast load, schema (`Product`, `Offer`, `AggregateRating`).

**Content format:** Comparison post, "best X" roundup, in-depth PDP, video demo.
**Conversion path:** Educational read → PDP → cart. Need 1–2 trust touches before buy.
**On-page priority:** comparison table, ingredient transparency, third-party testing claims, reviews, "for [use case]" framing.

**Conversion path:** Read → email signup or quiz → 14-day nurture → PDP. Conversion is delayed but high-quality.
**On-page priority:** clear answer in first 60 words (AI Overview slot), comprehensive coverage, citations, scannable headers, "Find My Ritual" CTA above the fold + at end.

## 4. Branded intent

**Mindset:** "I know Luma. I want to learn more / buy / find a specific product."
**Content format:** Homepage, PDPs, About, FAQ, reviews page.
**Conversion path:** Branded search is highest-converting traffic. Direct to most relevant page with minimal friction.
**On-page priority:** load speed, navigation, social proof above fold, clear "buy now" + "subscribe" paths.

## 5. Comparison / competitor intent

**Mindset:** "I'm choosing between Luma and a known competitor."
**Content format:** Long-form vs posts, fair/balanced comparison tables, audience-fit rec.
**Conversion path:** Read → quiz or PDP → cart. Educated buyer means fewer trust touches needed.
**On-page priority:** honest comparison table, real differentiators, ideal-buyer framing, single clear CTA.

| Keyword | Vol | Target page |
|---|---|---|
| luma daily vs olly | 30 | /blog/luma-daily-vs-olly |
| luma daily vs lemme | 40 | /blog/luma-daily-vs-lemme |
| luma daily vs goli | 30 | /blog/luma-daily-vs-goli |
| luma daily vs ritual | 20 | /blog/luma-daily-vs-ritual |
| luma daily vs hum | 20 | /blog/luma-daily-vs-hum |
| olly vs goli | 1,000 | /blog/olly-vs-goli (brand-positioned roundup, mention Luma as third option) |
| best wellness gummy brands | 880 | /blog/best-wellness-gummy-brands |

**Tactical note:** Comparison posts naming competitors are a high-leverage move. They:
1. Rank for the competitor's branded terms (free traffic from people researching them)
2. Capture demand at the bottom of the funnel
3. Position Luma Daily as the considered alternative

Run them from day 1, not day 90. Each comparison post should follow this structure:

1. 60-word answer paragraph (AI Overview bait): "*Luma Daily* and *[Competitor]* both make wellness gummies, but they differ in [3 key dimensions]."
2. Side-by-side comparison table (price, ingredients, formats, certifications)
3. "Best for" framing for both brands — concede where the competitor genuinely wins
4. Ideal Luma buyer description ("If you want a daily ritual rather than single SKUs...")
5. Quiz CTA + best-fit product CTA

## Intent → conversion path summary

| Intent | Volume share | Buying readiness | Optimal landing | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transactional | ~10% | hot | PDP / Bundle | Add to Cart / Subscribe |
| Commercial investigation | ~25% | warm | Comparison post | Compare → PDP |
| Educational | ~50% | cold | Pillar / Spoke | Quiz → email → nurture |
| Branded | ~10% | hot | / or PDP | Buy / Subscribe / Manage |
| Comparison (vs) | ~5% | warm-hot | vs blog | Quiz → PDP |

The 50% educational share is why content marketing is non-optional. Without it, we're competing for only the bottom 30% of intent — where competition + paid CPCs are highest.
