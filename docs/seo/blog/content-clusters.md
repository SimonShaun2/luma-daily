# Luma Daily — Blog Content Clusters

The blog is organized into **6 content clusters**, each with one **pillar page** (broad, ~2,500-word authority piece) and **8 supporting articles** (~1,200-1,800 words each, targeting specific long-tail queries that link up to the pillar).

Total content corpus at full build: **6 pillars + 48 spokes = 54 pieces**.

Architecture rationale: Google rewards topical authority. By publishing a comprehensive, internally-linked cluster around each topic, we signal to both search engines and LLMs that we're the definitive source for "daily wellness rituals" — which is the white space we're claiming.
