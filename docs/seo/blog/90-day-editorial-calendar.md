# Luma Daily — 90-Day Editorial Calendar

A day-by-day publish schedule for the first 90 days of the blog. Designed to ship pillars first (build topical authority fast), then layer in spokes (compound link equity within each cluster), then roll out comparison content (capture commercial intent in months 2-3).

**Total scheduled output:** 6 pillars + 24 spokes + 4 comparison posts + 2 ingredient deep-dives + 4 evergreen pieces = **40 articles in 90 days**.

This leaves the remaining 14 articles in the corpus to ship in months 4-6 as the evergreen / refresh queue.

## Publishing operating model

**Frequency:** 3 articles per week through day 90 (Mon/Wed/Fri primary, Sat optional).

**Lead time:** Each article needs 5-7 days of pre-production:
1. Day -7: assign brief (writer)
2. Day -5: outline approval
3. Day -3: draft delivery
4. Day -2: edit + brand voice review
5. Day -1: SEO + compliance review (titles, metas, schema, FAQ pairs, banned-term scan)
6. Day 0: publish + cross-link + share

**Roles needed:**
- 1 SEO editor (manages calendar, briefs, internal linking)
- 2-3 wellness writers (rotating assignments, ~2 articles each per month)
- 1 brand editor (voice + tone review)
- 1 compliance reviewer (FDA structure/function language, banned-term scan)

---

## Promotion cadence per article

Each new article gets the following promotion treatment in the 7 days post-publish:

- **Day 0:** Tweet/X thread with key insight + link
- **Day 1:** Instagram carousel (5-7 slides distilling the key takeaways)
- **Day 2:** Email to wellness list (subject line tested)
- **Day 4:** Cross-link from 2-3 existing articles
- **Day 7:** Reddit comment in r/Supplements with link (only when genuinely on-topic; avoid spam-flag)

This promotion regimen is what gets articles indexed fast and starts the link-flywheel.

---

## Refresh cadence

Every article should be reviewed at:

- **Day +30:** First refresh — title CTR check, expand if AI Overview slot underperforming
- **Day +90:** Second refresh — content audit, add internal links from newer articles
- **Day +180:** Third refresh — major content update, fresh data, expanded FAQs
- **Day +365:** Annual refresh — full rewrite if needed, repromote
